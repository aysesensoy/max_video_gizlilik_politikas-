Overview Max Video is a local video and audio player for Android and Windows. The app is designed to work offline. We do not sell your data, show ads, or charge for features. Information We Collect We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal data on our servers. Max Video has no user accounts and no backend servers operated by us. The app may store the following only on your device: Data Purpose Location App settings (theme, language, playback speed, battery saver, etc.) Remember your preferences Device storage (local) Library list (file names/paths you add) Show your saved media Device storage (local) Onboarding status Show introduction once Device storage (local) Your video and audio files remain on your device. We do not upload, scan, or analyze your media on external servers. Permissions (Android) The app may request: Read media / storage — to open files you choose Wake lock — optionally keep the screen on during playback (if you enable it in settings) These permissions are used only for app functionality. No internet permission is required for normal use. Internet & Third Parties The app does not require an internet connection to play media, manage your library, or trim files. We do not use advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or payment services in the app. We do not share your data with third parties. Children Max Video is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone. Data Security Because data stays on your device, you control it. Uninstalling the app removes app-specific local data. Your media files are not deleted unless you remove them yourself. Changes We may update this policy. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do.
